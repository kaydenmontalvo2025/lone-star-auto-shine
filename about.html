About Lone Star Auto Shine

At Lone Star Auto Shine, we take pride in delivering high-quality mobile auto detailing with attention to detail you can see and feel. What started as a passion for clean vehicles turned into a business built on hard work, consistency, and customer satisfaction.

We are a locally owned and operated business serving Mineral Wells and surrounding areas. Our goal is simple: provide reliable, professional detailing services that restore, protect, and maintain your vehicle—without cutting corners.

Meet the Owner

Lone Star Auto Shine is owned and operated by Kayden Montalvo, a local entrepreneur and college student who built the business with a focus on quality, consistency, and customer trust.

Every vehicle is personally detailed with care and attention to detail, using proven techniques and professional-grade products. As a locally owned business, accountability matters—when you book, you’re working directly with the owner.
